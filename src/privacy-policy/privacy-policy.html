Last updated: Nov 25, 2025

Introduction

HomeWorkx Rental Ltd (“HomeWorkx”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. By using our website or services, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your data when you:

  • Visit our website

  • Use any HomeWorkx services (buying, renting, leasing, project listings, verification, valuation, documentation, loans, renovation, etc.)
  • Interact with us on social media or physically at our office

Data Controller

HomeWorkx Rental Ltd is the Data Controller under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023. All inquiries about your data can be directed to the contact details at the end of this notice.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

Full name, email address, phone number, home or office address (if provided during transactions), identification documents (when required for verification or documentation).

Property & Transaction Information

Property interests or requests, documentation submitted for verification, lease, rent, or purchase data, service requests (e.g., renovation, valuation, loans, property management).

Technical Information

IP address, device type, browser type, pages visited and time spent on the website.

Financial Information (If Required)

Payment details or proof of payment, information needed for loan processing (submitted voluntarily). We do not store card information on our servers.

Social Media Information

If you interact with us on Instagram, Facebook, or X, we may receive profile details depending on your privacy settings.

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and manage HomeWorkx services
  • Verify property listings and user identities
  • Connect users with verified service providers
  • Improve user experience and website performance
  • Offer customer support
  • Process rental, purchase, loan, or service transactions
  • Provide legal/required documentation for property dealings
  • Send updates, notifications, promotional messages, and service improvements
  • Ensure compliance with Nigerian laws and prevent fraudulent activities

Legal Basis for Processing

  • Your consent (e.g., when submitting a contact form or resume)
  • Performance of a contract (e.g., property transactions, documentation, loan requests)
  • Legal obligation (e.g., property verification, compliance reports)
  • Legitimate interest (e.g., improving our services, preventing fraud)

Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to complete service transactions, comply with NDPA and other Nigerian legal requirements, resolve disputes, and maintain accurate business records. When no longer needed, data is securely deleted or anonymized.

Data Security

While no system is 100% secure, we take reasonable steps to protect your information. We use industry‑standard technical and organizational measures, including secure servers, encryption where applicable, access control and authentication, and regular security reviews.

Your Rights Under NDPA 2023

You can exercise these rights at any time:
  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of data where applicable
  • Restrict processing under certain conditions
  • Object to processing (e.g., direct marketing, profiling, legitimate interest)
  • Request data portability

Third‑Party Sharing

We will never sell your personal data. We may share data with verified service providers, financial institutions (with your consent), legal professionals, government agencies when required by law, and technology/security partners that help operate our website.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you by posting the new notice on this page. Please review periodically for changes.
