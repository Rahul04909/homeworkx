About HomeWorkx Inc.

HomeWorkx Inc. was created to fix the broken experience of property discovery and rental in Nigeria. We asked a simple question: what if buying, renting, leasing, and related services could be brought together in one secure, transparent, efficient and easy‑to‑use platform?

The Problem

For many Nigerians, finding a suitable property or renting one often feels like a journey without a map. You face endless calls to multiple agents, countless site visits, unreliable information, unexpected costs, and the constant fear of scams. Property owners ask the same questions: how do I find the right tenant, keep the property safe, and ensure proper management and profitability?

The Big Idea

What if property buying, renting, leasing, and related services could be centralized into one secure, transparent, efficient, and easy‑to‑use platform?

From that idea, HomeWorkx became a digital real estate platform that brings together buyers, renters, landlords, tenants, service providers, foreigners, and Nigerians in the diaspora who want to invest in the Nigerian real estate market or return home.

Today

HomeWorkx Inc. stands as a Nigerian‑registered, limited‑liability company offering a full suite of digital property solutions designed for convenience, safety, and efficiency.

What Sets Us Apart

We verify, secure, support, educate, and empower every user who interacts with the platform — we don’t just list properties. Our goal is to make each step clear, safe and compliant, so you can move forward with confidence.

Our Vision

To make property ownership, rental, and investment in Nigeria simple, transparent, and accessible, while becoming the country’s premier digital platform for secure and seamless real estate solutions.

Our Mission

To digitize and unify real estate services by delivering secure, affordable, and accessible solutions that connect buyers, renters, property owners, and businesses whilst empowering them to maximize their resources through a safe, efficient, and fully compliant platform.
